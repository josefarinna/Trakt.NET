    Enum TraktVideoType

    Determines the type of an video item.

    Namespace: TraktNET
    Assembly: Trakt.NET.Ex.dll
    Syntax
    public enum TraktVideoType

    Fields

    Name Description
    BehindTheScenes

    The video features footage of the cast and the crew on set during the production.
    Bloopers

    The video is a montage of mistakes and other amusing moments featuring the cast and crew during filming.
    Clip

    The video is any scene from a movie or show.
    Featurette

    The video represents any material that is not a trailer, a teaser, a behind the scenes or a clip.
    OpeningCredits

    The video features the on-screen title as well as the main cast and crew credits.
    Recap

    The video is a recap.
    Teaser

    The video is a teaser, which is usually shorter than a trailer.
    Trailer

    The video is a trailer.
    Unspecified

    An invalid video type.
